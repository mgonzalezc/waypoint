# Waypoint

An AI travel ranking app. You ask a question ("top 10 romantic restaurants in Rome"), Waypoint always answers with exactly ten, ranked, with the reason each one beats the next and the sources behind it. Never a star rating you have to take on faith.

## Why

Travel decisions are infrequent, expensive, and split across too many fragmented sources (Skyscanner, Booking, Tripadvisor, Maps). On top of that, review trust is eroding: a meaningful share of the reviews on major platforms are fake or incentivized. Waypoint's answer to both problems: a hard limit of ten results (fewer choices, not more), and a reasoned, sourced explanation for every ranking instead of an opaque score.
